# Product Strategy: The Great Canadian AI Trivia (Working Title)

## 1. Vision & Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

**Vision:** To deliver a premium, intelligent trivia experience that resonates with a Canadian audience by avoiding US-centric bias and offering endless variety through AI.

**Unique Value Proposition:**
1.  **Culturally Relevant (Not US-Centric):** We solve the frustration of Canadian players facing questions about obscure American senators or US state legislation. Our History and Politics categories are globally balanced or specifically Canadian-tailored, ensuring the content hits home.
2.  **AI-Powered Freshness:** Unlike static trivia decks that get stale, our AI engine monitors current events and trends to generate questions that are relevant *today*. Content is kept fresh and engaging automatically.
3.  **Theme on Demand:** Players aren't stuck with pre-set categories. They can select or even *request* themes (e.g., "Friends TV Show," "Organic Chemistry," "Toronto Street Food,") and the AI will auto-populate a unique trivia set instantly.

## 2. Target Audience

*   **Primary:** Canadians aged 18-50 who enjoy social gaming and want a game that speaks their language (metaphorically and culturally).
*   **Secondary:** Trivia buffs tired of US-defaultism in standard quiz apps.

## 3. Key Product Features

### Core Mechanics
### Core Mechanics
*   **Team Mode (The Default):** Trivia is a social sport. Groups can form teams (locally or remotely) to compete against others. "No one plays alone."
*   **Classic Mode:** Standard rounds of general knowledge suitable for solo or team play.
*   **Blitz Mode:** Fast-paced, timed rounds.
*   **Multiplayer:** Team vs. Team challenges, league play, and regional leaderboards (e.g., "Battle of the Provinces").

### The AI Advantage
*   **Dynamic Content Engine:** Generates questions in real-time based on selected themes.
*   **Relevance Filter:** Works in tandem with our **Content Designer** to ensure tone and difficulty are perfect.
*   **Difficulty Scaling:** AI adjusts question difficulty based on player performance.

### Theme Examples
*   **Canadiana (Dedicated Category):** "Canadian History," "Geography," "Local Legends."
*   **Global General Knowledge:** Science, Art, Literature (free from US-bias).
*   **Pop Culture:** "Schitt's Creek," "The Weeknd," "Friends," "Marvel," "Harry Potter."
*   **Niche Interests:** "Physics," "1990s Music," "World Cup History."

## 4. Technology & Execution
*   **Platform:** Web-based (PWA) and Mobile App (iOS/Android).
*   **AI Stack:** Leveraging LLMs for question generation and validation.
*   **Feedback Loop:** User rating system for questions to train the model on what represents "good" trivia.

## 5. Monetization Strategy (Draft)
*   **Freemium Model:** Core "Daily Canadian Challenge" is free.
*   **Premium Themes:** Access to unlimited AI-generated custom themes via subscription or token packs.
*   **Sponsored Events:** Partnerships with Canadian brands for branded trivia nights (e.g., "Tim Hortons Timbits Trivia").

## 6. Roadmap Highlights
*   **Phase 1:** MVP launch with General Knowledge + Canadian Classics themes.
*   **Phase 2:** Integration of "Theme on Demand" AI feature.
*   **Phase 3:** Multiplayer and Leaderboard implementation.
*   **Phase 4:** Creator tools allowing users to share their custom AI-generated decks.
